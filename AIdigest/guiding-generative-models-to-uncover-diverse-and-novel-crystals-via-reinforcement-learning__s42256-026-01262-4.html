Guiding generative models to uncover diverse and novel crystals via reinforcement learning

Hyunsoo Park · Aron Walsh (Department of Materials, Imperial College London) Published 2026-07-06 DOI 10.1038/s42256-026-01262-4 Source nature.com ↗
Core Conclusion

Likelihood-trained generative models are objective-misaligned with discovery: they reproduce the training distribution, whereas discovery needs the underexplored regions where novelty and stability coincide. The authors post-train a latent denoising diffusion model (Chemeleon2) with group-relative policy optimization (GRPO) and verifiable multi-objective rewards (creativity, stability, compositional and structural diversity). On the Alex-MP-20 benchmark this lifts mSUN from 15.9% to 61.3% (+45.4 pp; +285.5% relative), novelty from 62.3% to 97.5% and metastability from 51.2% to 72.1%, at a modest uniqueness cost (99.4% → 88.7%) — pushing the novelty–stability Pareto frontier outward rather than trading one for the other. The same reward machinery also beats classifier-free guidance for property-targeted (3 eV bandgap) generation while preserving chemical validity (mSUN 45.3% vs near-zero for CFG).

★

Why It Matters

  • It names and then breaks a general dilemma of scientific generative AI. The "novelty–validity trade-off" is not a crystals problem: any likelihood-trained generator is pulled toward the data it has seen. Showing that RL post-training moves the whole Pareto frontier rather than sliding along it is a claim that transfers to molecules, proteins, materials — and to spatial/urban design generators.
  • Rewards are verifiable, not learned preferences. Stability comes from a physics proxy (MLFF-predicted energy above the convex hull, validated against DFT), creativity from an isometry-invariant crystal descriptor (AMD), diversity from a kernel MMD in latent space. This is the "RL with verifiable rewards" recipe imported from reasoning LLMs into physical science, where the verifier is a simulator instead of a grader.
  • Latent space is the enabler, not a detail. Operating on (A, X, L) — discrete atom types plus continuous coordinates and lattice — makes policy log-probabilities intractable and roll-outs expensive. Compressing to a homogeneous continuous latent makes policy gradients well-posed and yields 3.8×–5.9× higher sampling throughput than the real-space predecessor.
  • Property targeting without heuristic guidance. Classifier-free guidance on a compressed latent collapses chemical validity (mSUN → ~0); RL keeps it (45.3%, rising to 60.8% after DFT relaxation) while concentrating the bandgap distribution on a deliberately out-of-distribution 3 eV target.
  • It is honest about reward hacking. Turning the diversity weight up broadens coverage (FMD−1 40.1% → 71.2%) but degrades metastability (40.9% → 32.2%) and pushes the policy toward chemically baroque quinary/senary compounds whose stability the sparse reference hull may overestimate — a documented failure mode, not a footnote.

GRPO draws G = 64 roll-outs per conditioning input with a batch of 5 conditions → 320 structures per gradient update; ε = 10−3, KL weight β = 1.0, entropy bonus γ = 10−5, AdamW lr = 10−5, early stopping on a 500-step reward plateau.
Validation samples
1,000 sampled structures cross-checked DFT vs MLFF energy above hull (errors < 0.10 eV per atom in the metastable window); t-SNE panels use 1,000 reference + 500 (Fig. 1d) or 1,000 generated (Fig. 4a,b) structures.
Reference thermodynamics
Convex hull built from Materials Project formation energies (GGA/GGA+U, uncorrected, for compatibility with foundational MLFFs); energies predicted by the MACE-MPA-0 machine-learning force field; metastability threshold Ehull < 0.1 eV per atom.
First-principles

Descriptors & filters

Model scale
Diffusion transformer: 12 layers, hidden dim 768, 12 attention heads; T = 1,000 DDPM training steps, 50 DDIM sampling steps; pretraining 5,000 epochs with EMA decay 0.99. VAE reconstruction accuracy 99.4% on the MP-20 test set (vs 85.5% for the ADiT transformer baseline).

Core Methods

  • Latent denoising diffusion as the policy. A VAE encodes (A, X, L) — atom types, fractional coordinates, lattice matrix — into a homogeneous continuous latent z; a 12-layer diffusion transformer denoises in that latent; the decoder (frozen during RL) reconstructs full crystals. Unlike ADiT, the VAE takes the lattice matrix rather than Cartesian coordinates, which is credited for the 99.4% reconstruction accuracy.
  • Reverse diffusion recast as an MDP. State st = (noisy latent, conditioning x, timestep); action = the predicted noise; policy = the denoising transition kernel pθ(zt−1|zt, x). Terminal reward evaluated on the decoded structure at t = 0.
  • GRPO instead of a critic. Group-relative advantage — reward z-scored within G = 64 roll-outs sharing the same conditioning — removes the value network, isolates conditioning-driven difficulty, and empirically shows lower gradient variance than REINFORCE (Fig. 2b). Clipped surrogate + KL to the pretrained reference policy + entropy bonus.
  • Four-term verifiable reward. Creativity: binary unique∧novel, smoothed for borderline cases by the Chebyshev distance between AMD vectors (k = 100) restricted to same-reduced-formula comparisons. Stability: −clip(EhullMLFF, 0, 1). Diversity: negative unbiased mixed MMD with a polynomial kernel over compositional and structural latents, credit-assigned per sample by a leave-one-out marginal-utility decomposition. Weights 1.0 / 1.0 / 1.0 and 0.1 for structural diversity.
  • Evaluation protocol. mSUN (metastable ∧ unique ∧ novel) as the headline metric, plus uniqueness, novelty, SMACT compositional validity, metastability, and coverage as FMD−1 on VAE structural embeddings; all measured on as-generated structures to avoid the flattering effect of post hoc relaxation.
  • Ablations that matter. GRPO vs REINFORCE (variance); with vs without the diversity reward (mode collapse into trivial geometric variants); diversity weight 0.1 vs 1.0 (coverage↑ 40.1→71.2% but metastability↓ 40.9→32.2%); RL vs CFG vs CFG+LoRA for property targeting; static vs DFT-relaxed bandgaps.

① Make the reward geometry-aware: from a global convex hull to a local feasibility field

Extending this paperThe stability reward is a scalar, context-free quantity: −clip(Ehull, 0, 1) at 0 K, referenced to a global Materials Project hull. The paper itself flags the consequence — for quinary/senary compounds the sparse hull overestimates stability, and the Ag2Au6Pd example is an ordered-supercell artefact of a solid-solution system. The extension is to make the reward a field rather than a number: condition the stability term on where in composition space the sample sits, weighting it by the local density and completeness of the reference hull (an epistemic-uncertainty discount), and adding a synthesizability/disorder term so that regions with thin thermodynamic support cannot be farmed for reward. Concretely, a calibrated MLFF ensemble variance becomes a per-sample confidence multiplier, and the policy is forbidden from converting ignorance into advantage.

Added value to my researchThis is precisely the failure mode of naive spatial-optimization objectives in my work: a facility-siting or EV-charging optimizer that maximizes a modelled accessibility score will happily exploit the places where the model is least constrained by data (rural cells with sparse mobility observations, tracts with unstable 2SFCA denominators). Importing the "uncertainty-discounted reward" pattern gives me a principled way to make R2SFCA-driven allocation risk-aware: catchment-level supply decisions weighted by the local epistemic confidence of the demand model, so optimization gains are not manufactured in data deserts. It also sharpens the explainable-ML agenda — the discount surface itself is an interpretable map of where the model should not be trusted.

② Close the loop the paper leaves open: RL post-training as an active-learning outer loop

Extending this paperChemeleon2's reward model is frozen during RL — MACE-MPA-0 and the bandgap surrogate never learn from what the policy discovers, even though the policy deliberately migrates into regions (transition-metal-rich, quaternary, polarizable-anion chemistries; 31.1% quaternary among RL outputs) where those surrogates were least trained. That is a self-defeating design: the better the exploration, the more off-distribution the verifier becomes. The natural extension is an alternating loop — GRPO explores, a small DFT budget is spent on the highest-uncertainty / highest-reward samples (the paper already runs VASP via Atomate2 for 1,000 validation structures), the MLFF and property surrogate are refit, and the policy resumes against a verifier that has followed it. This converts a one-shot post-training into a genuine closed-loop discovery engine and directly tests whether the mSUN gain survives an honest, continually re-grounded reward.

Added value to my researchThe same architecture is what my multi-source data-fusion work needs: a generator of candidate interventions (new clinic sites, telehealth hub placements, charger locations) proposing configurations, a cheap surrogate scoring them, and a budgeted expensive evaluation — an agent-based model run, a field survey, a mobile-phone-validated flow model — spent only where the surrogate is uncertain. It reframes "how many simulations can I afford?" as an explicit acquisition problem, and it makes the fine-tuning of geospatial foundation models iterative rather than a single supervised pass.

③ Port the "creativity reward" idea: continuous, isometry-invariant novelty for spatial objects

Extending this paperThe paper's cleverest single move is replacing binary novelty (StructureMatcher: match or no match) with a continuous, isometry-invariant descriptor (AMD, k = 100) that gives gradients on borderline cases. But the paper concedes that AMD-based novelty is still compositional in scope — comparisons are restricted to identical reduced formulas — and that "quantifying true structural novelty remains an open challenge" for low-symmetry structures and subtle lattice distortions. Extending it means building a prototype-level novelty metric: a symmetry-aware, distortion-tolerant embedding (Wyckoff-position or space-group-conditioned) whose distances reward genuinely new structural archetypes rather than new decorations of known ones. That would let the reward distinguish the paper's "controlled multi-element substitution" strategy from actual prototype discovery — a distinction the current mSUN metric cannot make.

Added value to my researchI have the identical measurement problem in urban form: is a neighbourhood configuration novel, or a translated/rotated copy of a familiar type? Building an invariance-aware descriptor for spatial configurations — invariant to translation, rotation and rescaling of the local street/POI arrangement, yet sensitive to genuine topological change — would upgrade my typology work from cluster labels to a continuous novelty coordinate, and gives spatial entropy a differentiable companion usable inside an optimizer rather than only in post hoc description.

④ Transplant the whole recipe to generative urban design: latent diffusion + GRPO over health-equity rewards

Extending this paperThe paper's own claim is that its contribution is modular and domain-agnostic: "a modular foundation for controllable AI-driven inverse design … across the scientific discovery applications of generative models." Testing that claim is itself an extension — the framework's three ingredients (a compressed continuous latent that makes policy gradients tractable, group-relative advantages that kill the critic, verifiable rewards that a simulator can compute) have never been tried on a domain where the "physics" is a social simulator rather than a force field. A crystal is a periodic arrangement of typed units under hard validity constraints; a neighbourhood plan is an arrangement of typed land uses under hard zoning, network and budget constraints. Running Chemeleon2's exact pipeline on urban configurations would tell us whether the novelty–stability Pareto shift is a property of the algorithm or of crystal chemistry.

Added value to my researchThis is the most direct import into my healthy-cities and 15-minute-neighbourhood lines: a latent diffusion generator of facility layouts post-trained with GRPO against a reward that stacks a 2SFCA accessibility term, a Gini/Theil equity term, a budget-feasibility term and a diversity term that stops the policy from proposing the same template for every city. Existing deep-RL urban planners search one site at a time; a diffusion policy proposes whole configurations and the group-relative baseline compares 64 alternative plans for the same neighbourhood — exactly the structure a planning workshop wants (a portfolio of distinct, defensible options, not one optimum).

⑤ Audit the reward-hacking boundary: turn the paper's cautionary note into a diagnostic instrument

Extending this paperThe paper reports a striking, under-exploited result: raising the structural-diversity weight from 0.1 to 1.0 nearly doubles coverage (FMD−1 40.1% → 71.2%) while metastability falls (40.9% → 32.2%) and the policy drifts toward chemically baroque high-order systems — an explicit case of reward hacking, diagnosed but only sketched. Extending it: sweep the four reward weights systematically, trace the resulting reward-weight → outcome manifold, and build an automatic hacking detector — e.g. flag when marginal reward is being earned mainly through elemental cardinality, or when generated samples cluster in composition regions with the fewest competing phases. Coupled with the entropy and KL terms, that yields a principled recipe for choosing reward weights rather than reporting the two that were tried, and an interpretable answer to "what did the policy learn to exploit?"

Added value to my researchEvery multi-objective spatial optimization I run — efficiency vs equity in EV charger siting, coverage vs cost in primary-care allocation — has the same hidden degrees of freedom, and the same danger that a weight choice silently determines the policy conclusion. A weight-sweep manifold plus a hacking detector is a directly transferable methodological product: it converts a subjective weighting decision into a reported sensitivity surface, and it fits the FAIR/open-science commitment of publishing the whole trade-off rather than the flattering corner of it. It also pairs naturally with explainable-ML attribution: which spatial covariates does the optimizer lean on when it starts gaming the objective?
