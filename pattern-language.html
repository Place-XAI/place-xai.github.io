A Pattern Language, visualized

All 253 patterns from Christopher Alexander's A Pattern Language (1977), each rendered as a single flat-vector illustration by generative AI and grouped into the book's three scales. The collection doubles as a study in theory-to-image fidelity — how faithfully a model can turn a written planning principle into a picture.
